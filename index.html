Where science stands in 2026: time (the 4th dimension of spacetime) is proven and measured every day. Extra space dimensions (5th–11th) appear in theories, but no experiment has detected one, and no machine can carry matter into one. This lab shows how to test that honestly.

02 · Why we can't go inside

Four reasons a human cannot step into a higher dimension

Each reason comes from a real theory and points at a different experiment. Together they explain why no "portal" has ever been seen.

A

It is curled up, tiny

A garden hose looks like a line from far away, but an ant can walk around it. A compact dimension of radius R needs momentum ħ/R to move in. For R = 10⁻¹⁹ m that is ~2 TeV per particle.

B

We are stuck on a brane

In brane-world models, the particles we are made of (electrons, quarks, light) are trapped on a 3D membrane. Only gravity leaks into the bulk. Walking cannot take you off the membrane.

C

Not every "dimension" is space

AI models use thousands of mathematical dimensions. A quantum state lives in a huge Hilbert space. Neither is a direction you can walk in.

D

Physics needs exactly 3

In 4 or more large space dimensions, gravity falls off as 1/r³ or faster. Planets spiral into stars and atoms collapse. The demo on the right shows it.

Orbit stability simulator

A planet starts on a near-circular orbit with a small push. Gravity in d space dimensions falls as F ∝ 1/rd−1.
